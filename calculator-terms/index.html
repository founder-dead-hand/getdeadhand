What It Does, And What It Doesn't

Dead Hand does arithmetic on numbers you give it. You set your labor rates, your material margins and your overhead; it applies them and produces a price.

It produces an estimate, not an invoice. It is not accounting software, it does not collect money, and it does not track what you are owed. Whatever you bill on stays in whatever system you already bill from.

The numbers are yours. We do not supply prices, rates, or a parts catalog, and we do not intend to. Every figure in your account is one you typed. You are responsible for whether your prices are right for your market, your costs, your licensing and your local law, including anything you are required to disclose to a customer. We do not check your math against anything, because there is nothing to check it against except your own numbers.

If a number comes out wrong because a number went in wrong, that is not something we can carry for you.
