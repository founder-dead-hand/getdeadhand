The Pricebook

The Book

The jobs you already know how to price, priced once and ready to pull.

The Book is open. You pick your shop size inside Dead Hand and it turns on for everyone on the login. Stepping up from the calculator replaces that subscription rather than adding to it, so you are never paying for both.

A dead-hand system fires a pre-set list of responses. The Book is that list.

The calculator handles the job you're standing in. Hours, materials, your margin applied while you type, a number in front of the customer in under a minute.

The Book handles the other kind. The water heater changeout you've done four hundred times. The panel swap. The after-hours call you could quote in your sleep and still get wrong once in a while, because it's nine at night and you've been up since five. Those jobs don't need working out. They need pulling.

So it isn't a feature bolted onto the calculator. It's a different job, on the same login.

What it isn't

It isn't our prices. We ship the shape of a job and leave every number blank. A book full of somebody else's prices is priced for somebody else's town, somebody else's overhead, and somebody else's payroll. We don't maintain a price list or a parts library, and we're not going to start.

It isn't an invoice. The Book makes an estimate. What you bill on stays wherever you bill from today. No receivables in here, no accounting sync, nothing new to reconcile at the end of the month.

It isn't a CRM. Same as the calculator. It stays a quoting tool.

Questions

Do you supply the prices?

No. We ship the structure of a job with every price left blank, and you fill in your own numbers. We don't maintain a price or parts library and we never will.

Is The Book an add-on to the calculator?

It's its own product on the same login. The calculator prices a job you're working out on the spot; The Book pulls a job you've already priced. Different job, different tool. Stepping up from the calculator replaces that subscription rather than adding to it, prorated, so you never pay twice.

Is it priced per truck?

No. Flat by shop size: $30 for one truck, $80 for two to five. Adding a tech inside your band doesn’t change the price. Above five trucks we do not sell a bigger Book — that is what Friendly is for.

Can my techs use the same book?

Yes. One login for the shop, priced by the number of trucks you run. Your crew pulls from the book you set up.

Does it produce an invoice?

No, an estimate — a clean PDF or an export you carry into whatever you invoice with. There's no receivables side to this and no accounting sync.
