Why It's Called Dead Hand

Built to get it right when you can't.

The name comes from an old fail-safe idea: a system built to respond correctly on its own, at the exact moment the people who'd normally make the call aren't able to. No hesitating, no second-guessing — it just does what it's set up to do.

Your version of that moment is the kitchen table.

You're tired, you're moving fast, the customer's waiting, and the number you say in the next thirty seconds is the one you're honoring tomorrow. That's exactly when a good tradesman talks himself into a price that's too low.

Dead Hand takes that off your shoulders.

Your rates, your margin, your overhead — set once, then applied automatically to every job after. The right number comes out even when you're too slammed to work it out in your head.

It gets the number right,
so you don't have to be perfect on your worst day.

The Problem

The kitchen table is where jobs get lost.

Quote too high, they call the next guy. Quote too low, you eat the difference all week. Scratch it on a pad and half the time you've forgotten the margin by the time you write the invoice.

You've done this in your head for years and you're good at it. But the customer wants to see the breakdown — a document, not a guess. And you'd rather be the one who hands them something clean than the one digging through the truck for a clipboard.

How It Works

Four taps from walking in to quote delivered.

01

Enter Hours

How long is the job. That's it. Your rates are already set from the first time you opened it. No dropdown, no "which labor type," no picker.

02

Add Materials

Tap, type the cost. Tap again for the next one. Your margin's already applied and the total updates as you go. Reorder or remove with a swipe.

03

Present to the Customer

Flip the phone around. Presenter mode shows the number big and clean — subtotal, tax, total. Settled right in front of them. No loading screen, no ads.

04

Send the PDF

One button. Their email, from your phone's share sheet. No login for them, and the only thing that ever leaves your phone is the quote you chose to send.

What It Isn't

This isn't a CRM. It's a tape measure.

Dead Hand does one thing: quote fast, quote right, send clean. It won't turn into a dispatch board or a customer database you have to feed. If you ever need those, that's a different product — this one stays sharp.
